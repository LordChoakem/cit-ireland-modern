About CIT Ireland

Over 40 years of expertise in precision manufacturing from our facility in Abbeyfeale, County Limerick.

Our Heritage

Established in 1982 to serve the European and Asian electronic and lighting component markets, CIT Ireland Limited builds on origins dating back to 1956 as Glass Tite Industries Inc. We have perfected techniques in glass-to-metal sealing, cold heading, wire forming, welded leads and component cleaning.

Located at Mountmahon Industrial Estate, Abbeyfeale, we occupy 24,000 square feet and are ideally positioned with convenient access to Shannon, Cork, Kerry and Dublin airports.

Our Products & Services

High-volume production of precision components for demanding industries.

Glass to Metal Seals

Specialists in high-precision glass-to-metal and glass-to-glass sealing for electronics, aerospace and lighting applications. Decades of expertise in hermetic sealing technology.

Cold Heading

Billions of precision and micro-formed cold headed components. Wire diameters 0.076mm–1.52mm, lengths 0.25mm–75mm. Materials include Copper, Brass, Kovar, Dumet, Stainless Steel and more.

Wire Forming & Precision Pins

Proprietary cutting and micro forming technologies. Diameter range 0.10mm–2.0mm, lengths 1.0mm–300mm. Multiple forms, straight cut, beveled or die rolled ends.

Welded Leads

High-quality welded lead wires for electronic and electro-mechanical applications, produced with our advanced manufacturing processes.

Component Cleaning

Specialised component cleaning processes ensuring the highest standards of cleanliness and surface integrity for critical applications.

Additional Capabilities

Actuators, Pusher Beads, Precision Cuts and Formed Pins, Wire Straightening, and Glass Tube Cutting. Flexible manufacturing to meet custom requirements.
